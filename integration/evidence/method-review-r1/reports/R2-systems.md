## Supported contributions and mechanism assessment

**S1. The three mechanisms are complementary obligations of a profiler, not arbitrary module names.** M1 supplies the entity and surviving source origin to which a record refers. M2 supplies the control semantics and trustworthy publication of that record. M3 connects the record to the actual launch and separates identity from clock alignment. Removing any obligation permits a different false conclusion. `paper/sections/04-design.tex:19–59,63–108,117–162` provides this structure; the implementation has corresponding behavior. Evidence anchor: **text**, “Source mapping, invocation identity, and clock alignment are separate obligations.” This is a useful organizing argument, although the abstract idea of provenance or normalized events is prior work.

**S2. GCU contains a substantive, nontrivial implementation lesson.** Selecting one work-item is insufficient when lowering distributes the words of the logger's vector value. Numerical correctness alone accepted the broken logger. The repair validates complete scalar publication or a complete record in each work-item ownership slice. The actual counterexample and controls are recorded in `artifacts/method-run/enflame/GCU-I3-evidence.md`, with raw before/after archives and final focused tests; `execution_enflame.py:197–446` exposes actual symbolic ownership and partial origins. Evidence anchor: **dataset**, those before/after publication archives and full36 vector audit. This supports public knowledge about validating instrumentation's own lowering, not a claim that selective instrumentation or backend-specific recording is new.

**S3. Ascend association crosses genuine compiler/runtime/SDK boundaries.** `CannLaunchReceipt.h` retains a per-launch token with lifetime callbacks and original worker-local query statuses; `cann_native.py:_verify_source`, `_verify_dispatch`, `_compute_valid` check retained receipts, capture seals and compute-table membership. This is materially stronger than name/order matching. Mixed dispatch/context views are preserved without duplicate kernel costs. Evidence anchor: **dataset**, `integration/evidence/ascend-static-readiness-20261015/mix-hivm-root-independent-audit-r27.json` and `integration/evidence/cann-mixed-context-20261015/r25-review-handoff.json`, together with repeated/two-stream hardware evidence. The current support does not generalize to arbitrary compound compute membership or unconstrained concurrent detailed launches.

**S4. The negative results are valuable evidence rather than automatic disqualification.** Small Ascend RMSNorm remains +61–68% all-site and +60–78% wait-only; GCU vector publication trades compilation feasibility for +26–27% selected-site perturbation. Long-K has low native-task perturbation but high collection/export costs. The paper states these distinctions. Evidence anchor: **dataset**, `ascend-final-closure-20261015/longk-root-cost-sample-audit-r29.json` and GCU controlled T5 records; **text**, evaluation lines 54–84. An offline diagnostic tool can still be useful at these costs. A general low-overhead/default-all-kernels claim is not supported.

M1 is a **partial execution/origin manifest**, not complete transform-preserving source-operation cost attribution. `ascend_hivm.py:16–165` verifies same-compile actor evidence and creates only unique typed static cross-actor channels; it does not pair dynamic flag generations. M2 is real dynamic logging of supported control points, not completion/busy measurement; `AscendLateObserver.cpp:125–210` brackets existing primitives and inserts no extra completion barrier. Program selection gates publication after timestamp reads. M3 provides real invocation association and local duration conversion, with certified shared source/native origin unavailable. These mechanisms solve a useful **bounded version** of the cross-architecture profiling problem. They do not solve arbitrary-kernel profiling, global overlap/critical-path reconstruction, exclusive op costs, or uniform timing capability across devices.

They do not present the tool output → decision → disabled-tool outcome chain. **Evidence anchor: absence:** checked `03-study.tex`, `06-evaluation.tex:90–109`, `Profiler/test/enflame_method_fidelity.py`, GCU I3 evidence, Ascend static-readiness/final-raw/final-closure reports; missing final-service diagnostic comparison and documented decision derivation.

This is not a claim that the Study is invalid or that the formal tool lacks all relevant information. There is promising diagnostic signal in actual implemented output. The paper currently leaves the reader to infer its usefulness.

Show actual tool fields which identify ownership/exchange, actor readiness, changing command/checkpoint counts and local wait behavior. If retained captures do not support a required link, run only that bounded formal-entry comparison under the next freeze. Native timing alone and native timing plus ordinary IR/vendor information are reasonable information baselines; no cross-device port of Proton/KPerfIR is required.

**Predicted verification:** another reader can derive the stated intervention from the displayed fields without a Study-specific LLVM rewrite or unreported manual measurement. If the tool cannot expose the decisive relation, add that specific generic extraction, or narrow the claimed role. ### W2 — Byte/identity fidelity and clock-rate conversion do not establish diagnostic timing fidelity

**Severity: Major. Class: evidence gap. Confidence: 4/5 — source and actual control records inspected.**

**Claim → anchors:** Evaluation calls its first subsection “Measurement and Attribution Fidelity,” but its listed matrix oracles establish math, buffer integrity, association and conversion. Lines 39–42 explicitly defer event coverage, region association and timestamp accuracy. `cann_source_clock.py:_compiled_logger/_local_rate` verifies rate provenance and local semantics; it cannot establish uninstrumented wait accuracy. `AscendLateObserver.cpp:159–182` reads clocks in other programs even when they do not publish. **Evidence anchor: text:** evaluation lines 39–40, “Validate event coverage, region association, timestamp accuracy”.

Whole-task perturbation of roughly 1% does not bound error in a particular wait or issue bracket. Instrumentation can redistribute waiting between actors or shift scheduling while task duration remains similar. Conversely +60% small-kernel overhead is a concrete reason to doubt untreated fine-grained cost attribution there, even with correct timestamps. This is a known issue in [KPerfIR §5.3/Figure10](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi25-guan.pdf), which explicitly discusses distorted asynchronous wait measurements; the current paper must demonstrate its chosen interpretation rather than assume that honest labels resolve the empirical question.

**Finite repair:** define a small independent semantic/timing oracle: controlled existing wait/no-wait paths, known loop trip counts and branch visits, plus counter-read/empty-bracket floor at actual resolution. On the decisive fused case, compare all-site versus wait-only or another sparse supported request and an independent activity observation; inspect generated instructions/resources sufficiently to explain discrepancies. Judge stability of the diagnostic conclusion and intervention direction, not a manufactured universal percentage-accuracy target. Keep zero-tick events as below-resolution control brackets. Report sampled-site/count coverage independently of retained-buffer decoding.

**Predicted verification:** the same producer/readiness diagnosis persists under the supported observation variants, or the paper states the condition under which it fails. No shared epoch is required to compare local duration distributions. If new hardware is needed, Ascend/GCU access already used for this task suffices; NVIDIA clean performance remains the user's explicitly deferred item and must stay unresolved rather than becoming a reason to rerun it now.

### W3 — The public systems knowledge needs a sharper argument than integration success

**Severity: Major. Class: expression gap with a bounded evidence gap. Confidence: 4/5 — contribution and prior-work comparison.**

**Claim → anchors:** Intro contribution 2 and `07-experience.tex:9–40` provide the three useful failures, but primarily recount them. Evaluation's test/matrix totals dominate the evidence for the service; there is no compact account of what an operator developer can now safely decide, what a profiler implementer should change, and which assumptions the new environments invalidate. **Evidence anchor: text:** experience lines 17–18, “Trace validation must cover the actual writers and committed record bytes”. This lesson is sound but the paper should show its actionable boundary and broader consequence.

[KPerfIR](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi25-guan.pdf) already supplies compiler-centric scopes, portable instrumentation, bounded buffering, asynchronous-profiling analysis and an AMD-specific collaborative recording strategy. [Proton's official CGO abstract](https://2026.cgo.org/details/cgo-2026-papers/23/Proton-Towards-Multi-level-Adaptive-Profiling-for-Triton) establishes adaptive/multilevel/custom-metric profiling; full paper access remains unavailable, so absence of more specific features is unverified. [PASTA §III-G](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22103v1) normalizes semantics/timing metadata through vendor-specific handlers. Thus “different backends need different adapters” and “preserve semantics” do not by themselves expand knowledge enough. The exact GCU ownership failure, Ascend worker/context identity boundary, and selection-versus-perturbation separation are the stronger story.

**Finite repair:** organize the operational account around these falsifiable assumptions, each with the observed failure, actual repair, residual restriction, and reusable validation recipe. Add a short truthful description of the controlled developer workflow and its cost: how requests are chosen, when compilation is prohibitive, what requires a replay, how unsupported/ambiguous results change decisions, and what is retained for offline diagnosis. Much exists in the records already; do not invent users, duration or maintenance history. Integrate the concrete two-case evidence from W1 into that account.

**Predicted verification:** a reader can state which assumptions failed in the new environments and how to test the same failure in another compiler/profiler. This can make the contribution proportionate to Operational Systems without a new profiling IR or an invented production history. If revision can only restate existing generic profiling practices and cannot show consequential new-environment failures, then a new impactful diagnostic use case or genuine operational use revealing further systems lessons is the minimum new condition; more adapter counts will not help.
