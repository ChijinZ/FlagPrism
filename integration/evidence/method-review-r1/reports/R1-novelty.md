## Judgment

**The implementation contains substantial, credible systems work. Its three mechanisms could support an OSDI Operational Systems submission, but the present method draft does not yet make that contribution sufficiently self-contained and convincing.** I would request major revision before treating it as submission ready. I do not find a demonstrated structural impossibility or a need to invent a new profiling IR. Nor would production-user count be a defensible rejection criterion by itself.

The strongest contribution is narrower than a new heterogeneous profiling architecture: **what breaks when established compiler instrumentation and correlation techniques cross vector ownership, split compiler actors, and asynchronous SDK workers, and which evidence makes the resulting observations trustworthy.** The retained failures and repairs give this claim substance. “Three mechanisms,” schema richness, and three adapters are not independently novel. The public knowledge lies in counterexamples, operational boundaries and validated remedies in these environments.

A short implementation/text/evidence iteration can resolve the concrete provenance mismatch below, clarify prior-work positioning, and turn existing results into a materially stronger Operational Systems argument. It cannot, by itself, establish a practical low-perturbation mode for short Ascend kernels, certify a shared actor clock, prove broader adoption, or guarantee OSDI acceptance. Those require new conditions only if the authors choose to promise those capabilities.

## Primary-source comparison

I read the official sources, rather than inferring features from the packet's descriptions.

* **KPerfIR already owns much of the generic story.** Sections 4.2–4.4 and 5 describe compiler insertion, backend lowering, hidden profile-buffer arguments, raw records, iteration semantics and producer/consumer analysis; Section 5.2 provides bounded circular storage. Its Figure 6 and FA3 discussion already connect compiler structure to fine-grained overlapping and optimization. Thus “mapping program semantics to runtime observations,” fine-grained pipeline diagnosis, and buffered dynamic traces cannot distinguish this work by themselves. Its first-thread export mechanism is relevant context for the candidate's ownership counterexample, not evidence that KPerfIR has the same GCU defect. [KPerfIR, OSDI 2025](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi25-guan.pdf), Sections 4–6.
* **Proton is not merely a coarse NVIDIA profiler.** The official CGO abstract describes selective regions, custom metrics, vendor-plus-instrumentation backends and levels extending to compiler IR. The author LLVM talk describes generic and target-specific lowering, mode selection, sampling/granularity, circular recording and compiler-linked pipeline tracing. These remove adaptive instrumentation, multi-backend architecture and compiler-aware intra-kernel profiling from the exclusive contribution set. I could not read the full CGO paper; the retained finite search records that access limitation. Absence from accessible slides does not establish absence from Proton. [CGO official abstract](https://2026.cgo.org/details/cgo-2026-papers/23/Proton-Towards-Multi-level-Adaptive-Profiling-for-Triton), [author LLVM talk](https://www.jokeren.tech/slides/LLVM25_slides.pdf), slides 5–17; [author HPCA talk](https://www.jokeren.tech/slides/HPCA25_slides.pdf), slides 10–11 and 21–29.
* **PASTA explicitly normalizes semantics and timing metadata.** Section III-G also retains specialized handlers for vendor features; Sections III-E and IV describe connecting framework information with native events. A common event vocabulary, vendor extensions and cross-layer association in the abstract are prior. The candidate's defensible comparison is its actual compiler actor/channel extraction and specific worker/context identity proof, not a claim that PASTA lacks semantics. This is a scope distinction, not a proven priority claim. [PASTA primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22103v1), Sections III-E–III-H and IV.

The current Related Work (`paper/sections/08-related-work.tex:5–30`) has substantially corrected overclaiming. However, lines 13–15 still introduce a broad “meaning of feedback” contrast that KPerfIR's actual iteration and dependency examples also address. Replace that contrast with the exact target-boundary differences and mark unverified feature comparisons as unverified. A cross-device port or performance benchmark of Proton/KPerfIR is unnecessary for this repair.

## Findings and finite repairs

### R1-1 — Major; implementation gap, with an expression consequence: source-hash validation is stronger in the text than the readiness importer

**Claim:** `paper/sections/04-design.tex:45–47` says the adapter validates captured stream and source hashes before certifying the channel.

**Source:** `ascend_hivm.py:32–42` checks receipt completion, print flag, stream truncation/hash and a nonempty compile ID, then merely copies `source_ir_sha256`. There is no requirement that this field exist or match the retained source snapshot. Artifact LLVM hashes and matching compile IDs are checked at `:45–52`. `ascend_sdk_driver.py:32–39` genuinely computes a source hash, but that is a producer action, not consumer validation against the retained source. The supplied fixture at `Profiler/test/test_ascend_hivm.py:28–68` has no source hash and is expected to become `compiler_static`. `execution_ascend.py:182–195` separately checks a tracked-table hash for origin matching; it does not close this SDK-source comparison.

**Consequence:** Normal audited hardware captures can be correct, and I found no evidence that they are mismatched. Nevertheless, an incomplete or inconsistently assembled handoff can receive the same readiness status as a fully source-bound handoff. This matters to the claimed evidence contract; it is not proof of a wrong readiness edge in the recorded experiment.

**Repair:** Require the SDK receipt's source hash to match the exact retained pre-driver source bytes/snapshot and require that snapshot to belong to the enclosing handoff compile ID. Pass that expected evidence explicitly into readiness construction, or narrow the exported state/text if only same-job actor binding is intended. Do not treat two self-declared matching strings as a rehash of retained source bytes.

**Predicted verification:** Docker negative contracts for absent source hash, changed retained source, wrong source hash and stale enclosing compile ID must produce unavailable/partial readiness; current genuine retained M1 and long-K bundles should retain their existing static channel after byte revalidation. No new performance campaign is needed.

### R1-2 — Major; expression/evidence gap: the manuscript has not yet distilled the Operational Systems contribution

**Anchors:** Introduction `:81–95` lists study, implementation and validation; Experience `paper/sections/07-experience.tex:5–7` still requests actual workflow/duration/maintenance context; Evaluation `:95–109` compresses all mechanism checks to a few assertions. The stronger counterexamples and limitations live in external evidence. OSDI explicitly says the paper must stand alone and supplement reading is optional. [OSDI 2027 CFP](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi27/call-for-papers), Submission Tracks and Supplementary Material.

**Problem:** As written, a reviewer can reasonably see “known profiler architecture, three ports, many tests.” The alternative interpretation—three concrete failures of reasonable assumptions and the general conditions under which their repairs work—is credible but insufficiently demonstrated in the paper. Passing counts and exact hashes establish execution provenance, not impact or transferable knowledge by themselves.

**Repair using available material:** Center the contribution around (1) a numerically correct kernel yielding corrupt logger words because logger ownership differs from publisher selection; (2) a caller-thread correlation failure and duplicate dispatch/compute views repaired by worker/context evidence; (3) event reduction failing to reduce small-kernel cost. For each, show the failed assumption, one actual artifact/output before and after, the invariant, supported scope, and remaining failure mode. Include one concise actual developer workflow using the formal public entry, with what diagnosis was possible and its collection cost. Report real research-machine context and dates; do not invent users or production deployments.

**Predicted verification:** A reader should reconstruct the counterexample and understand why the repair follows without opening the engineering log. Existing retained captures appear sufficient for most of this. If no actual formal-tool workflow led to an optimization decision, that causal claim must remain absent; a new observed workflow would be the minimal new condition for making it.

### R1-4 — Major; evidence/product gap for broad practicality, not an automatic novelty veto: cost has a narrow usable regime

**Anchors:** `paper/sections/06-evaluation.tex:43–88`; `longk-cost-audit-r29.json`, `comparisons`; packet's GCU full-matrix losses; original requirements `method_goal_prompt.md`, Section 7.

The existing negative results are substantive: small RMSNorm detailed overhead is 61.2–67.6%, wait-only remains 60.1–78.0%, and the long-K example's low device change coexists with roughly 12.4 ms host calls, 15.6–17.2 s export and 221–229 MB sessions. GCU scalar selected-store device cost is modest, but vector selected-store is about 27%; the full vector matrix drops 21,996 records. None invalidates correct bounded observations. They do prevent “generally practical low-overhead profiler” from being concluded.

**Repair:** Define the supported use as costly, selective offline diagnosis where that is the measured regime. Present retained event coverage/loss, compile time, host delay, export and storage beside device perturbation; include what useful question can still be answered with each mode. Do not optimize by quietly changing the measured workload or selection. Native-off and clean NVIDIA costs remain missing measurements, not negative proofs.

**Predicted verification:** Existing raw summaries can close much of the reporting work. A broadly usable short-kernel mode would require a new mechanism or independently validated lower-cost collection path, followed by the same paired correctness/identity/cost checks. A clean NVIDIA measurement window is the minimal external condition for certifying its costs. Neither is necessary to publish a candid negative systems lesson, but both are necessary for broader performance promises.

## Operational Systems decision boundary

The official CFP does not require new research ideas; it asks for design, implementation, analysis and experience that expand public systems knowledge, including known techniques validated in new environments. Its deployment examples are not a numeric user quota. I therefore assess the candidate as **partly meeting**, not categorically failing, this track. Three research machines are a limited but real operational context, and compiler/SDK boundary failures can yield public knowledge. The work must demonstrate that knowledge directly, rather than treating effort or device diversity as a substitute. [OSDI 2027 CFP](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi27/call-for-papers), Submission Tracks.

My engineering-validity judgment is favorable within the stated tested envelope, subject to R1-1 and the documented performance limits. My paper-contribution judgment is provisional: the strongest version is an evidence-rich systems implementation/analysis paper about trustworthy profiling across mismatched execution and runtime boundaries. A broad novel-profiling-method paper is not supported by the available prior-work comparison.

I recommend a bounded iteration on the identified hash contract and on self-contained counterexamples, final-tool diagnostic walkthrough, contribution wording and cost reporting, followed by fresh review. If that iteration still leaves only three local fixes without a demonstrated useful workflow or transferable inference, more implementation polish will not fix the contribution deficit: the minimal new condition would be a real observed use yielding a nontrivial diagnostic result, or a systematic experiment establishing a boundary rule across materially different mappings/runtime conditions. It need not be a production deployment, a new user quota, or a cross-vendor port of prior tools.
