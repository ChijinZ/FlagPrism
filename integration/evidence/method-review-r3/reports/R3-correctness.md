## Findings

### M1 — Major, bounded implementation gap: unresolved competing readiness sites can be ignored when certifying uniqueness

**Claim → code → counterexample.** `paper/sections/04-design.tex:53` says missing or multiple candidates remain partial. `Profiler/python/flagtree_profiler/ascend_hivm.py:220–234` groups all typed sites by flag and pipelines, but counts only sites already labeled `verified_same_compile_actor`. Consequently an unresolved additional producer disappears from the uniqueness test.

I reproduced this with the existing `test_ascend_hivm.py` fixture: append another post-GraphSyncSolver AIC function named `unretained_producer`, containing `sync_block_set[<CUBE>, <PIPE_FIX>, <PIPE_S>] flag = 7`, while retaining only the original producer and consumer LLVM artifacts. The source bytes, stderr hash, enclosing compilation and both retained artifacts remain valid. The parser emits three sites: original producer verified, consumer verified, additional producer `unassociated_actor`. Nevertheless it returns `state='compiler_static'` and a producer→consumer channel for flag 7. This is not proof that the unresolved competing producer belongs to a different invocation or channel. It contradicts the stated conservative partial-evidence rule.

**Impact and severity.** This is a real partial-import correctness gap, not evidence that the frozen hardware diagnostic channel is wrong. It matters because rejecting unsupported/incomplete evidence is part of the method's promised service. I classify it Major for that contract, with high confidence in the reproduced behavior and bounded confidence about its frequency in real SDK output. It is not a structural contribution deficit or a demand for broader kernel support.

**Finite repair.** Before certifying a channel, require exactly one raw typed set candidate and one raw typed wait candidate for the channel scope, with both uniquely bound to verified actors. Alternatively, introduce and validate a stronger compilation/dispatch scope that proves why an unresolved competitor belongs elsewhere. Preserve unresolved sites and a reason in partial output. Merely weakening the prose would be insufficient if the export still presents an unconditional unique dependency.

**Validation.** Add targeted CPU contracts for a missing or rejected-artifact competing set, competing wait, and duplicate/ambiguous actor membership alongside a valid pair. Each must withhold the affected channel. Keep the existing unique-pair, different-pipeline and duplicate-static-site contracts passing. Reparse the already retained real unique-channel capture to show unchanged accepted output. No new hardware is necessary.

## Operational Systems contribution versus acceptance

The [CFP's Operational Systems criterion](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi27/call-for-papers) permits public systems knowledge from implementation, analysis and experience without requiring a new research idea. It does not waive significance, correctness or a convincing account of practical benefit. Here the positive case is the tested compiler/runtime/SDK system and concrete qualification lessons in new execution environments.

The prior-art boundary matters. [KPerfIR §§5.2–5.3](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi25-guan.pdf) already addresses backend-specific collaborative stores and distorted asynchronous waits; [Proton's abstract](https://2026.cgo.org/details/cgo-2026-papers/23/Proton-Towards-Multi-level-Adaptive-Profiling-for-Triton) already includes selective regions, custom metrics and vendor/instrumentation unification. PASTA's primary §III-G already normalizes event semantics/timing through vendor handlers. Thus no general invention of semantic profiling, selective instrumentation, hidden buffers, multivendor support or perturbation awareness is established. The manuscript's related-work section appropriately credits these foundations.

The narrower contribution remains plausible: the concrete GCU ownership failure and complete-record repair, same-compilation typed AIC/AIV channel provenance, retained-worker and SDK compute-context identity, and an honest decision workflow despite failed timing/cost assumptions. This exceeds mere interface breadth. Its impact is finite and retrospective; whether that is sufficiently substantial for OSDI is uncertain, rather than something supplied by passing 469 tests.

A finite implementation/evidence iteration can resolve my concrete correctness concern. No unavailable structural condition, production deployment, new backend, new feature catalog, shared-clock implementation or new hardware experiment is required to preserve the current contribution. Conversely, broadening the claim to accurate local costs, automatic discovery, general low overhead or deployment impact would require evidence not available in this candidate; a wording change could not establish those stronger claims.
