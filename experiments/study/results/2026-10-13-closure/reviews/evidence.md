## Remaining issues

**Acceptance-blocking gap, criterion 5:** exact internal numerical precision is unproven. CUPTI/aclprof/TOPSPTI whole-kernel/task durations are independent endpoint/collection paths, but containment does not compare the same internal region against ground truth. Fitting a slope and outside-bracket intercept also cannot isolate internal endpoint error, timer-read cost, completion-fence perturbation or hardware oscillator independence. GCU official helper frequency remains null. The q16/q256 holdouts only check the fitted whole-task scale relation and were partitioned after exploratory data existed. Needed to close the precision subrequirement: an appropriate independent measurement of the same intended internal region, or a separately justified error bound that explicitly accounts for timer quantization/read/fence effects. Do not label this as fully verified internal accuracy.

**P1 metadata consistency:** preserved raw CANN `result.json` semantics contain old phrases such as softmax “Original flags delimit completed input transfer and vector compute”; fused completion raw semantics still refer to an added PIPE_MTE2 split. These historical fields conflict with the current issue/gate downgrade and final PIPE_ALL fences. `endpoint-policy-corrections.json` and current paper provide the correct interpretation. Reproduction consumers should resolve that correction explicitly, rather than trusting raw `semantics` alone. This is not a numerical-data contradiction.

**P1 reproducibility provenance:** selected GCU summary `source_math_preserved` says only clock declarations/callees and library target changed, while final adapter also guards record staging/publication. Mathematical work is preserved, but the description should name the publication guards; writer-audit already documents them. Integrating exporter/provenance updates may resolve this.

No observed mixed-field rate is established for CANN generic fused aliasing; the current paper correctly calls it a static two-writer alias proof. No production support, cross-actor overlap, unperturbed busy-time or generic profiler product failure should be inferred.
